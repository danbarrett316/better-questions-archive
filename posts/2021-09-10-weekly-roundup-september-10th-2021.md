## **Five Interesting Links For The Weekend:**

[**you are beloved and worthy of rest**](https://annehelen.substack.com/p/you-are-beloved-and-worthy-of-rest?ref=betterquestions.co)

I enjoyed this essay, but for whatever reason, the mantra of "You are beloved and worthy of rest" has deeply lodged into my mind. I've been repeating it to myself.

[**Tyler Cowen is the best curator of talent in the world**](https://kulesa.substack.com/p/tyler-cowen-is-the-best-curator-of?ref=betterquestions.co)

I'm a Tyler Cowen fan, but knew very little about his investment in upcoming talent. This is a fascinating look at how Cown cultivates connections. His philosophy on how he works through this process is very similar to what I wanted to do with *Better Questions*: if I simply write for well-meaning, earnest, and smart people, can I gather more of them around me?

[**How I experience the web today**](https://how-i-experience-web-today.com/?ref=betterquestions.co)

Brutal, funny, true. Marketers ruin everything (and yes, I work in marketing)

[**Stop Death Shaming**](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2021/09/stop-death-shaming/619939/?ref=betterquestions.co)

Found this article to be pretty spot on. Compare/contrast with [this Better Questions essay from last year.](https://www.betterquestions.co/one-in-a-million/)

[**I found the heaviest distortion pedal and used it on harp**](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNINWMqxQQc&ref=betterquestions.co)

Absolutely wonderful. This is why the internet exists.
