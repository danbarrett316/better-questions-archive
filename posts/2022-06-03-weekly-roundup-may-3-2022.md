## **Five Interesting Links For The Weekend:**

**Dangerous messaging around a recent SIDS study - [https://peterattiamd.com/dangerous-messaging-around-a-recent-sids-study/](https://peterattiamd.com/dangerous-messaging-around-a-recent-sids-study/?ref=betterquestions.co)**

In an earlier issue I linked to the whole "now we know what causes SIDS thing," and in the process forgot one of my personal rules: *never share a link about science you don't understand (which is all links about science, because I am not a scientist.)*

**If you can't take in anymore, there's a reason - [https://thecorners.substack.com/p/if-you-cant-take-in-anymore-theres](https://thecorners.substack.com/p/if-you-cant-take-in-anymore-theres?ref=betterquestions.co)**

Stressful news is stressful, and we aren't really emotionally equipped to handle it. Schedule time and space to engage with what you can and don't beat yourself up.

**What I learned as a hired consultant for autodidact physicists - [https://aeon.co/ideas/what-i-learned-as-a-hired-consultant-for-autodidact-physicists](https://aeon.co/ideas/what-i-learned-as-a-hired-consultant-for-autodidact-physicists?ref=betterquestions.co)**

Really interesting article with two big takeaways (for me):

We don't know what we don't know, which creates the illusion that we know more than we do - and,

There really is an audience for every business model.

**No Meetings, No Deadlines, No Full-Time Employees - [https://sahillavingia.com/work](https://sahillavingia.com/work?ref=betterquestions.co)**

Cool article about how Gumroad works. Made me rethink some assumptions.

**Tom Cruise’s Last Stand - [https://www.vulture.com/article/tom-cruise-top-gun-maverick.html](https://www.vulture.com/article/tom-cruise-top-gun-maverick.html?ref=betterquestions.co)**
