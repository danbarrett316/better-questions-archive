# Narcissism, Social Media, and Mimetic Desire

An arc that builds through the blog and explodes in late 2022, addressing the cultural machinery of desire, identity, and control. Barrett argues that social media doesn't just waste time — it fundamentally colonizes our desires, replacing authentic wanting with manufactured resentment, and that the only response is radical renunciation. This theme evolves into broader concerns about AI, the post-trust internet, and the survival of authentic human connection.

---

## Core Concepts

### Mimetic Desire (Rene Girard)
- We want what others want. Desire is not spontaneous but imitative.
- "We tend to want what we think others want." ("The Critical Path," 2022)
- Self-discovery requires limiting outside influence — specifically, reducing mimetic contamination.
- The first step in Barrett's Critical Path framework: "Reduce mimetic desire."
- Social media is a machine for manufacturing mimetic desire at scale.
- ("I Finally Did It," 2022; "The Critical Path," 2022; "Renunciation," 2022)

### Narcissism as Cultural Foundation
- "Narcissism isn't just the problem, it's the foundational belief of our entire culture." (Christopher Lasch, *The Culture of Narcissism*)
- The narcissist needs an admiring audience and cannot stand alone.
- Social media is the narcissism engine: "it's agonizingly difficult for me to pick up the phone and call my friend."
- "It's easier to feel bad about yourself than it is to change." — This is itself a narcissistic maneuver: self-pity as a substitute for action.
- Even critiquing narcissism is narcissistic: "How can I hold up a mirror without simply becoming enamored of my reflection?"
- ("Not The First Time," 2022)

### Resentment Masquerading as Desire
- "You do not want what you say you want." ("Act Now," 2022)
- "You don't have dreams, you have defenses. You don't have desires, you have resentments. You don't have enemies, you have hallucinations."
- "Wanting to be wanted" takes the place of actual wanting — a cowardly avoidance of risk. ("Wanting To Be Wanted," 2022)
- Resentment is the dominant emotion of our culture, disguised as aspiration.
- Depression as narcissistic: "Depression plunges the subject into itself. Eros, in contrast, pulls the subject out of itself, toward the Other." (Byung-Chul Han, *The Agony of Eros*)

### The Control Mechanism
- Cultural context determines which questions we get to answer.
- "The Other controls us by (1) limiting what we think is possible, and (2) teaching us how to want."
- "If I control what you want — and I control what you think is possible — I control you."
- Social media cannot be mediated or resisted — only renounced.
- Alignment or opposition to the system both constitute control by the system.
- "Get the f--- off social media. Now."
- "You will read this, nod, resolve to do better, and you will do nothing."
- "There is no you. Not anymore."
- ("Renunciation," 2022)

### Defense Mechanisms and Self-Deception
- Freudian dream censorship: our real wishes are hidden from us by defense mechanisms.
- "We find it easier to want things that we are told to want — that we don't want, but that there can be no guilt in wanting because they were commanded to be wanted." (Edward Teach)
- "Ambiguous and vague goals protect our egos. By defining success, we are also defining failure." ("The Critical Path," 2022)
- "Smart people, in particular, build their self-images around their talent, intelligence, and skill. Failure is perceived as threatening that self-image."
- The dare: "Find the heaviest boulder you can find, push it up that hill, and accept the responsibility for what you find at the top." ("Act Now," 2022)

### Psychofauna and the Post-Trust World
- **Psychofauna:** Entities arising from human minds that act upon us in the real world — AI bots, engagement algorithms, recommendation engines.
- Modern media consists of entities, not objects. CoCo Melon is engineered to colonize children's attention. Twitter is increasingly populated by unknowable AI agents.
- "The age of humanity being largely free from natural predators is over. We have created them, and they are here — and they are hunting us." ("The Psychofauna Age," 2025)
- **"Psychoanalysis in reverse"** (Rick Roderick): modern media turns the conscious into the unconscious.
- "You cannot form a social contract if you are perpetually unsure of who you are talking to."

### The Post-Trust Internet
- "Post-trust: some content being fake spoils the experience of all content." (Gresham's Law applied to information)
- "Those who can build trust will also build power." ("How I Use AI," 2025)
- AI used as editor, not content producer. "I never have and I never will use AI to write these blog posts."
- In a post-trust world, connection is what makes information influential.
- Trust-building as long-term power strategy.

### The Return to the Real
- Barrett's 2022 mission statement: pulling away from the virtual, exploring authentic desires, building real connections.
- "I feel more and more disconnected from reality. I feel more and more disconnected even from my internal sense of the world. Everything feels thin and fake." ("Channeling The Other," 2022)
- The resolution: renounce social media, act despite fear, stop hiding behind defenses, return to the real.

### The Critical Path (4-Step Personal Growth Framework)
1. **Reduce mimetic desire** — limit outside influence to discover what you authentically want.
2. **Define the gap** — name what success looks like (which also names failure, an act of courage).
3. **Create internal feedback loop** — weekly journaling, quarterly review.
4. **Own your shit** — share failures publicly; destroy ego; attract feedback; expose yourself to others' struggles.
- ("The Critical Path," 2022)

## The Arc of This Theme

**2021:** Implicit. Barrett quits social media ("The Product Is You") using the framework of subtraction and behavioral systems. The critique is personal, not cultural.

**2022 (first half):** Mimetic desire enters through reading Rene Girard. The Critical Path framework formalizes "reduce mimetic desire" as Step 1. The critique deepens but remains constructive.

**2022 (second half):** The explosion. Reading Edward Teach triggers the most confrontational, darkest posts in the blog's history. Narcissism is identified as the culture's operating system. Social media is declared beyond reform. The call is for radical renunciation. "Act Now" is the existential dare.

**2023-2024:** The theme quiets as Barrett explores psychoanalysis and hypnosis more deeply. But the cultural critique remains in the background.

**2025:** The theme returns through the lens of AI. Psychofauna, the post-trust internet, and AI safety extend the social media critique into an existential territory. The resolution: authentic human connection and trust-building become the scarce resources in a world of manufactured content.

## Connections to Other Themes

- **The Unconscious / Psychoanalysis:** Narcissism and mimetic desire are understood through psychoanalytic concepts — defense mechanisms, repression, the metonymy of desire
- **Epistemology / Map-Territory:** Social media creates false maps of reality that colonize our territory; manufactured desire replaces authentic knowing
- **Power / Meta-Game:** The post-trust world makes trust-building a power strategy; understanding mimetic desire is understanding the meta-game of culture
- **Self-Acceptance:** The trance of unworthiness makes us vulnerable to mimetic desire and narcissistic identification; self-acceptance is the precondition for authentic desire
- **Mortality / Existential:** "You are fighting for your life. Did you forget?" — The urgency comes from the awareness that time is finite and wasted by manufactured desire
- **Behavioral Systems:** Social media is a system that produces behavior; renunciation is a system design choice (removing friction toward bad behavior by removing the platform entirely)
