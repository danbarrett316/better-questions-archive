# Daniel Barrett's Intellectual Index — Master Guide

A comprehensive map of the major themes, ideas, frameworks, and thinkers that define Daniel Barrett's body of work across the Better Questions blog (2020-2025), PPC philosophy, and agency steering documents. These indexes are designed to serve as roadmaps for future content creation — providing context, direction, and intellectual grounding.

---

## The Ten Themes

### [01 — Systems Thinking and Complexity](./01-systems-thinking-and-complexity.md)
The foundational framework. Cynefin, Theory of Constraints, feedback loops, emergence, "you can never do just one thing," drift into failure. The world is composed of interconnected systems whose behavior cannot be predicted by examining their parts.
**Key thinkers:** Dave Snowden, Eliyahu Goldratt, Donella Meadows, Garrett Hardin, Sidney Dekker

### [02 — Epistemology and the Map-Territory Problem](./02-epistemology-and-map-territory.md)
How we know what we know — and the systematic ways our knowledge fails. General Semantics, Bayesian reasoning, falsificationism, the "Just Perfect" heuristic, abstraction diseases, Goodhart's Law. Every belief is a limiting belief.
**Key thinkers:** Alfred Korzybski, Karl Popper, Thomas Kuhn, Thomas Bayes, Gerd Gigerenzer

### [03 — Risk, Uncertainty, and Decision-Making](./03-risk-uncertainty-and-decision-making.md)
Risk (knowable odds) vs. uncertainty (unknowable odds). The Turkey Problem, risk of ruin, heuristics, MinMax Regret, micromorts, via negativa, optionality. The volume/quality oscillation trap in PPC.
**Key thinkers:** Nassim Taleb, Gerd Gigerenzer, Stephen Jay Gould

### [04 — Statistical Process Control and Variation](./04-statistical-process-control-and-variation.md)
The measurement backbone. All numbers vary. Routine vs. exceptional variation. XMR/PBC charts. The 5-step discipline. The OODA loop as operating method. Voice of the Process. Alpha generation.
**Key thinkers:** W. Edwards Deming, Donald Wheeler, Taiichi Ohno

### [05 — Behavioral Systems and Personal Change](./05-behavioral-systems-and-personal-change.md)
Barrett's Rule: "No goals without systems." The five behavioral systems (pre-commitment, stakes, friction, feedback, social pressure). Subtraction over addition. Ironic process theory. The Gemba Walk. The Personal Danban. Root cause analysis.
**Key thinkers:** W. Edwards Deming, Nassim Taleb, Eliyahu Goldratt, Donella Meadows, Clifton Mitchell

### [06 — Mortality, Impermanence, and Existential Freedom](./06-mortality-impermanence-and-existential-freedom.md)
Death as the background of responsibility. Constraint as liberation. The impermanent self. Anxiety and finitude. Failure as universal. Radical freedom. "Everything is a choice."
**Key thinkers:** Viktor Frankl, Jean-Paul Sartre, Joan Didion, Oliver Burkeman, Ernest Becker

### [07 — Empathy, Communication, and Influence](./07-empathy-communication-and-influence.md)
Empathy as the foundation of all communication. Tactical empathy. NVC. The three errors of interpersonal perception. Crucial conversations. Perception/Context/Permission. Weak ties.
**Key thinkers:** Chris Voss, Marshall Rosenberg, Chase Hughes, Keith Johnstone, Mark Granovetter

### [08 — Power, Strategy, and the Meta-Game](./08-power-strategy-and-the-meta-game.md)
Common knowledge games. The missionary. The Keynesian Beauty Contest. Information cascades. Status as a learnable game. The ethics of power. The war of attrition in PPC.
**Key thinkers:** Keith Johnstone, John Boyd, Ben Hunt, Martin Gurri, Eugene Ionesco

### [09 — The Unconscious, Psychoanalysis, and the Mind](./09-the-unconscious-psychoanalysis-and-the-mind.md)
The predictive brain. Dual-process cognition. Hemispheric theory. Freud and Lacan. The trance of unworthiness. Hypnosis. Dissociation as healing mechanism. Self-acceptance.
**Key thinkers:** Andy Clark, Iain McGilchrist, Sigmund Freud, Jacques Lacan, Tara Brach, Milton Erickson

### [10 — Narcissism, Social Media, and Mimetic Desire](./10-narcissism-social-media-and-mimetic-desire.md)
Mimetic desire. Narcissism as cultural foundation. Resentment masquerading as desire. The control mechanism of social media. Psychofauna. The post-trust internet. The Critical Path. The Return to the Real.
**Key thinkers:** Rene Girard, Christopher Lasch, Edward Teach, Byung-Chul Han

## How These Themes Interconnect

The ten themes form an integrated intellectual system, not isolated topics:

```
EPISTEMOLOGY (02) ←→ SYSTEMS THINKING (01)
     ↕                      ↕
RISK/UNCERTAINTY (03)    SPC/VARIATION (04)
     ↕                      ↕
BEHAVIORAL SYSTEMS (05) ←→ MORTALITY/FREEDOM (06)
     ↕                      ↕
EMPATHY/INFLUENCE (07) ←→ POWER/META-GAME (08)
     ↕                      ↕
THE UNCONSCIOUS (09) ←→ NARCISSISM/MIMESIS (10)
```

**The vertical axis** moves from the abstract (epistemology, systems theory) to the personal (psychology, self-acceptance) to the cultural (narcissism, social media).

**The horizontal axis** moves from understanding (left) to action (right) — from knowing to doing.

**The throughline:** We live in complex systems (01) that we cannot fully map (02), producing uncertainty (03) that we manage through measurement (04) and system design (05), all within the constraint of mortality (06), navigating relationships (07) and power dynamics (08), while our unconscious minds (09) are colonized by cultural forces (10) that distort our desires and perceptions.

## Usage Notes for Content Creation

These indexes are designed to be brought into any content creation session to provide:

1. **Intellectual grounding:** Every piece of content should connect to at least one of these ten themes.
2. **Consistency of voice:** The signature concepts, quotes, and mental models ensure new content sounds like Barrett, not like generic marketing.
3. **Cross-pollination:** The best content emerges at the intersection of themes — e.g., applying SPC (04) to the meta-game (08), or connecting existential freedom (06) to behavioral systems (05).
4. **Citation depth:** Each index includes the specific thinkers, works, and quotes that Barrett draws on, preventing content from drifting into generic territory.
5. **Evolution tracking:** The themes evolve over time (e.g., the psychoanalytic turn of 2022, the General Semantics emergence of 2024-2025). New content should build on the latest synthesis, not regress to earlier formulations.
